Guangyu (Noah) Shen

I am a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. I work on identifying and mitigating misaligned behaviors in frontier models.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Purdue University, advised by Prof. Xiangyu Zhang. My doctoral research spanned AI safety and security, from discovering attack vectors such as backdoors and jailbreaks to developing mitigations through safety alignment and guardrails. I also led efforts in real-world AI security challenges, achieving leading performances in the IARPA TrojAI Challenge and the Amazon Nova AI Challenge.
