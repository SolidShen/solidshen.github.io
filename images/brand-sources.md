# Brand asset sources

- `trojai-logo.png`: NIST TrojAI leaderboard, https://pages.nist.gov/trojai/public/images/trojaiLogo.png
- `nova-ai-challenge-logo.png`: Amazon Science's Nova AI Challenge page, https://www.amazon.science/nova-ai-challenge (official CDN asset named `amazonnovaaichallenge-updated-logo.png`).

The originals are retained. CSS displays the horse emblem and Nova star respectively as small decorative marks after the corresponding names.

- `openhands.svg`: https://www.openhands.dev/assets/webflow/brand/openhands-logo-mobile-on-dark.svg (official OpenHands mark; white fill changed to dark blue for the light page background).
